# Three product walkthroughs

[Portfolio](../README.md) · [English](tour.md) · [简体中文](tour.zh-CN.md) · [日本語](tour.ja.md)

## 01 / Follow the evidence

Open [AiSeek](https://aiseek.dev/en), choose a published change or dossier and inspect its supporting passages. Use the [method page](https://aiseek.dev/en/method) to understand the evidence treatment; the [correction record](https://aiseek.dev/en/corrections) shows how published material can be revised.

**Look for:** a path from the statement to its support.

## 02 / Move from a story to its context

Open the existing [SeekMap](https://aiseek.dev/en/seekmap), choose a country and explore the associated changes. Follow an entity into the available structure views, then inspect the claims behind a relationship.

**Look for:** geography and relationships that help navigate the information, with hypotheses distinguishable from established facts.

## 03 / Turn reading into a routine

Read a [brief](https://aiseek.dev/en/brief), inspect the [Ahead calendar](https://aiseek.dev/en/ahead), or sign in to follow a subject. For tool integration, open the [developer guide](https://aiseek.dev/en/developers) and use the API or MCP with your account's key and limits.

**Look for:** a reason to return, and a clear route from reading to follow-up.
