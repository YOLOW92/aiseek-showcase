## The reader's problem

Important changes arrive across languages, outlets and institutions. Readers need more than another feed: they need to know what changed, why it matters, which claims have support and what deserves another look.

## The product

AiSeek organises important changes across technology, economics, policy and other domains. Its reading experience connects context, evidence, entities and follow-up. Research supports that experience and provides deeper tools for eligible users.

| User task | Product capability |
|---|---|
| Understand a change | Changes and contextual dossiers with source passages |
| Explore connections | Structure views, entity context and the existing SeekMap |
| Keep following | Follows, alerts, briefs and release calendars |
| Use another tool | Read-only API and MCP with account limits |

The product supports English, Chinese and Japanese. Public methods, corrections, contact and credits make its treatment of information inspectable.

## Next direction

**AiSeek 2.0 is planned.** SeekEarth aims to give the changing world a more coherent geographic and temporal view. SeekTrace aims to make evidence records and their history easier to follow. These directions address the need for a memorable product centre and clearer continuity of evidence; they are not part of the delivered capabilities above.
