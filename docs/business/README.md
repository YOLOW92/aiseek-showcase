## The opportunity

Information is plentiful; **auditable context is scarce**. News feeds answer what was published. Research teams, investors, founders and curious professionals also need to know whether a change is significant, which organizations or technologies are connected, what can be checked against primary evidence, and what remains only a hypothesis.

AiSeek attempts to make that workflow available through one multilingual product. This is a **product thesis**, not evidence that the market has been won.

## Differentiation to test

| Alternative | Typical strength | AiSeek's proposed distinction |
|:--|:--|:--|
| News aggregators / RSS readers | Fast collection and breadth | Explicit evidence trail and entity-level structure |
| Search engines / AI answers | Flexible querying and synthesis | Persistent, classified claims tied to locators and corrections |
| Financial terminals / research platforms | Mature specialist datasets and workflows | Accessible multi-domain context across technology, policy and companies |
| General-purpose knowledge graphs | Connected entities | Source-linked, time-aware change monitoring |

These are **category comparisons**, not an exhaustive competitor benchmark or a claim that rivals lack these capabilities. AiSeek's differentiators remain hypotheses until measured with real user research and retention.

## Monetization explored by P72

- **Individual plans:** tiered access to reading, research and related features; billing infrastructure used Polar.
- **Developer access:** a read-only API, an MCP interface and embedding options under defined entitlements.
- **Organizational partnerships:** bespoke data/research workflow discussions; no public enterprise revenue is claimed.
- **Product distribution:** multilingual site, newsletter, public channels, developer experience and sharing.

**No audited revenue, paid conversion rate, customer count, ARR or investment return is claimed** in this portfolio. Prices and entitlements may change; consult [the live plans](https://aiseek.dev/en/pricing).

## What has been de-risked — and what has not

| Demonstrated by the P72 product | Still needs independent validation |
|:--|:--|
| Deployable multilingual interface and production workflow | Sustainable acquisition and long-term retention |
| Ingestion, provenance rules and relationship analysis | Comprehensive factual accuracy across all subject areas |
| Research, subscriptions and developer interface plumbing | Reliable willingness to pay and positive unit economics |
| Operational guardrails around model costs and source rights | Scalability under large customer loads and contractual source licensing |

### On traffic and growth evidence

Cloudflare edge requests and edge-classified page views **must not be presented as human users**. The available edge and browser-side measures differ substantially and are not directly interchangeable. This release intentionally **omits visitor and growth headline figures** rather than implying they have been reconciled. The historical data figures elsewhere are identified as *product content inventory*, not audience or revenue.
