## My role

I initiated AiSeek and carry the work from product direction to operation. The project is a practical record of how I turn an ambiguous idea into requirements, make technical tradeoffs, inspect results and keep revising the product after release.

| Responsibility | What I own |
|---|---|
| Product | User problem, positioning, reading flow and priorities |
| Technical direction | System boundaries, quality rules and resource constraints |
| Delivery | Work decomposition, review, acceptance and release decisions |
| Operation | Corrections, production issues, iteration and business direction |

## From idea to production

![Product journey](../assets/diagrams/journey.jpg)

| Milestone | The important decision |
|---|---|
| 3 October · foundation | Establish shared contracts and an application that can be run and inspected. |
| 6 October · reading experience | Bring product clarity, mobile layout and language into the reading journey. |
| 7 October · evidence and SeekMap | Make supporting material visible and give changes geographic context. |
| 10 October · quality and search | Correct misleading baselines and address retrieval across scripts. |
| 12 October · return and reliability | Add return paths, measurement foundations and browser error reporting. |
| Next · planned | Give AiSeek 2.0 a clearer centre through SeekEarth and evidence continuity through SeekTrace. |

The direction also evolved from a research-first entry point toward an information product about important changes. Research stayed as a supporting capability and a deeper tool.

## Development method

AI coding tools participate in implementation. I set direction and constraints, organise the work, review outcomes and decide acceptance.
