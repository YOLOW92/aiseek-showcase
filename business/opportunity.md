## The opportunity

People following industries, companies and policy spend effort joining scattered updates, checking the support and deciding what to revisit. AiSeek's business hypothesis is that a coherent, multilingual path from change to evidence can make that work easier and worth paying for.

## Who the product is for

| Audience | Need | Product path |
|---|---|---|
| Readers | Understand an important change quickly | Free discovery and public evidence |
| Ongoing trackers and researchers | Follow subjects and build context over time | Subscriptions, alerts, briefs and deeper tools |
| Developers and product partners | Read structured changes in another workflow | API, MCP and integration |

## The differentiation to examine

The product joins changes, source passages, entity relationships, geography and follow-up in one experience. English, Chinese and Japanese are first-class reading surfaces. The opportunity is in this connected workflow.

For a partner evaluating the work, the practical questions are: does the reader reach useful evidence, can a subject be followed, and can another tool consume the output? The [product walkthroughs](../product/tour.md) expose those paths.

## Revenue paths

Free discovery leads to paid continuity and deeper use. Developer access connects the product to external workflows. Individual sponsorship and resource support can help sustain development; partnerships can explore distribution and useful integrations. [Current plans](https://aiseek.dev/en/pricing) describe the paid product.

## Current stage and next validation

AiSeek is running, with subscriptions and read-only developer access implemented. Paying demand, retention and distribution efficiency still need to be demonstrated with reviewed data.

The next questions are whether readers return for the evidence-linked workflow, whether subscription use is repeatable, and whether integrations solve an ongoing task. [Measurement plan](../proof/status.md) · [Partnership brief](partnership.md)
