## What exists to work with

A running multilingual product, evidence-linked reading, follows and briefs, developer interfaces, and an operator who owns the product direction and delivery. The engineering and product sections show the work behind these capabilities.

## Collaboration paths

| Path | A useful starting point | What success would mean |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution | Introduce a relevant audience to a specific reading path | Readers reach useful evidence and return |
| Product integration | Connect a repeatable task through API or MCP | The integration is used beyond its first demo |
| Resource or data support | Support a constrained capability with clear usage rights | Better delivery within a sustainable cost |
| Sponsorship | Support independent development | Continued, visible product progress |
| Investment | Discuss the product thesis and the next validation work | A shared plan grounded in reviewed evidence |

I am interested in a concrete problem, a bounded pilot and a way to evaluate the result.
