## Selected work / AiSeek

**A multilingual information platform for understanding what changed, why it matters, and where the evidence comes from.**

AiSeek brings important changes, their context and their sources into one reading experience. Readers can explore connections, follow subjects and return through alerts and briefs. The main product is available in English, Chinese and Japanese.

I initiated the project and own its product direction, requirements, engineering constraints, acceptance decisions and operation. This portfolio focuses on the main information platform.

## Try the work

Start with a change on [AiSeek](https://aiseek.dev/en), then inspect its supporting sources. Explore the connections, follow a subject, or read a brief. For integrations, see the [developer guide](https://aiseek.dev/en/developers).

| Reader need | Delivered product surface |
|---|---|
| Understand a change | Changes, contextual dossiers and source passages |
| See connections and places | Structure views and the existing SeekMap |
| Come back with a purpose | Follows, alerts, briefs and release calendars |
| Connect another tool | Read-only API and MCP, with account limits |

These are capabilities documented in the product's release history, checked against the source revision recorded in the [evidence notes](proof/README.md). They are not claims of measured accuracy, traction or revenue. Availability and current terms belong to the live product.
