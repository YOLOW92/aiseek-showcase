## Measurement that matters next

| Question | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Are people discovering the product? | Human traffic separated from bots, then reading and sign-up paths |
| Do readers return? | Cohort return and repeat follow-up use |
| Is the paid value repeatable? | Verified subscriptions, revenue and continued paid use |
| Is the information useful? | Reviewed support and relevance samples, corrections and user feedback |
| Do integrations solve ongoing tasks? | Repeat API/MCP use tied to a partner's actual workflow |

This is the validation agenda for the next stage. The current business brief rests on the deployed product and its implemented paths.
